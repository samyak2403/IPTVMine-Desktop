Engineered for Performance & Restraint

Every detail is designed to feel native, responsive, and completely respectful of your privacy.

🔒

100% Local-First Privacy

No accounts, no cloud sync, and zero tracking. Your playlists, history, and preferences stay strictly inside local SQLite on your own PC.

📺

Bring-Your-Own Sources

Import M3U, M3U8, and IPTV JSON playlists directly from URLs or local files. Easily manage custom User-Agents and channel categories.

⚡

HLS Player & External Handoff

High-performance built-in video player with instant keyboard shortcuts, plus seamless 1-click launch to external players like VLC or mpv.

📥

Resumable Offline Downloads

Integrated background download manager with pause/resume support and automatic crash recovery on startup.

🌑

Pure AMOLED Black Canvas

Apple-crafted OTT interface with smooth spring physics, custom borderless window shell, and a true #000000 OLED theme option.

🛡️

Encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS

Bypass ISP DNS blocks natively with built-in DoH resolvers (Cloudflare, Google, AdGuard, or custom endpoints) and live latency checks.
